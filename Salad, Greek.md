# Greek Salad

Dressing

- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 0.5 **lime** *(juice from)*
- 1-2 cloves **garlic** *(minced)*
- 1 tsp **honey**
- 1 tsp dried **oregano**
- 0.5 tsp ground black pepper
- 0.25 tsp kosher salt

Salad

- 0.25 **red onion** *(diced)*
- 12-16 oz **mini cucumbers** *(diced)*
- 1 large green or red **bell pepper** *(diced)*
- 4-8 oz **cherry tomatoes** *(halved)*
- 3 oz **feta cheese crumbles**
- 4-8 large leaves **fresh mint** *(very thinly sliced)*

Instructions

1. Mix together **dressing**
1. Prepare **salad** ingredients
1. Mix salad with dressing to combine
